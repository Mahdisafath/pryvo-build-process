# Result-report template

This is a reusable blank template. Fill it with observed facts, not assumed success.

## Requested outcome

What did this task set out to establish?

## Change made

What changed and why was that change necessary?

## Verification

| Date | Setting | Check performed | Observed result | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Actual date | Local, testnet or another stated setting | Concrete check | Passed, failed or skipped | What this does not establish |

## Remaining work

What is incomplete, blocked or still untested?

## Decision needed

What choice, if any, must be resolved before the next action?

## Handoff

Where can the reviewed artifacts be found? What is the next bounded task?
