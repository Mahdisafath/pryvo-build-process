# Contributing to the teaching materials

Suggestions about clarity, reusable templates and the synthetic example are welcome.

A useful suggestion identifies the confusing passage, explains why it was hard to follow and proposes a clearer version. Distinguish your own example from an actual result obtained during the Pryvo build.

Keep contributions focused on the materials in this repository. Do not add product implementation files, private records or another person's contact details.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution can be shared under this repository's MIT license.
