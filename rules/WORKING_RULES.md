# General rules I give an AI builder

These are newly written, reusable summaries of my working standards. They are not a transcript or a copy of the product's implementation instructions.

1. **Understand what exists.** Inspect the relevant work before proposing a rewrite.
2. **Use a written target.** Describe the requested outcome and the limits of the task.
3. **Surface uncertainty.** Identify missing choices and contradictions before relying on assumptions.
4. **Work in reviewable steps.** Make each result small enough to understand and check.
5. **Verify the requested behavior.** A description of a change is not evidence that it works.
6. **Report the setting.** Distinguish a simulation, a testnet action and a plan.
7. **State the limits.** Report failures, skipped checks and what remains untested.
8. **Keep the next step explicit.** Do not silently expand the task after completing it.
9. **Preserve the reasoning.** Record the problem, the choice and the reason so the next session can continue.
10. **Make claims match the evidence.** “Passed these checks” is more precise than a broad claim about the whole product.

The motto is **vibe coding with security checks**. It describes attention to verification and review. It is not a statement that AI-generated work is secure or independently audited.
